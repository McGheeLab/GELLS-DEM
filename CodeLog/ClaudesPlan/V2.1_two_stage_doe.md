# V2.1 Two-Stage DOE Strategy

## Problem
Full factorial 5^7 = 78,125 runs is infeasible (~1.4M CPU-hours, 9× monthly Puma allocation).

## Solution: Two-Stage Adaptive DOE

### Stage 1 — Latin Hypercube Screening (500 runs)

**Goal:** Map the full 7D parameter space efficiently. Identify which factors dominate each
response and locate promising regions.

**Design:**
- **Method:** Maximin Latin Hypercube Sampling (scipy.stats.qmc)
- **Runs:** 500 (~70 runs per factor, good for quadratic response surfaces)
- **Factors:** 7 continuous, sampled uniformly within bounds:

| Factor | Key | Min | Max | Units |
|--------|-----|-----|-----|-------|
| A | E_modulus | 2 | 50 | kPa |
| B | phi_solid_target | 0.55 | 0.85 | — |
| C | func_ratio | 0.2 | 1.0 | — |
| D | cell_surface_coverage | 0.5 | 1.5 | — |
| E | cell_sense_distance | 20 | 80 | µm |
| F | R_func_mean | 30 | 150 | µm |
| G | R_inert_mean | 30 | 150 | µm |

**HPC estimate:** 500 × 4 CPUs × ~4.5 h ≈ 9,000 CPU-hours (6% of monthly Puma allocation).

**Analysis after Stage 1:**
1. Fit quadratic response surface models for key responses (porosity, compaction, bridges,
   permeability, architectural distance)
2. Compute Sobol sensitivity indices — rank factors by importance
3. Identify "dead" factors (contribute <5% variance) — fix at midpoint for Stage 2
4. Locate response surface features: optima, saddle points, steep gradients, phase boundaries

### Stage 2 — Adaptive Refinement (200–400 runs)

**Goal:** Add resolution where the response surface is interesting — steep gradients, near
optima, or near organ-matching targets.

**Design:** Generated automatically by `generate_doe_stage2.py` which:
1. Loads Stage 1 results and fitted response surfaces
2. Identifies the top 3–4 factors (Sobol indices)
3. Generates refinement points using one or more strategies:
   - **Near-optimal:** Dense sampling around the best runs (within 10% of best response)
   - **Steep-gradient:** Fill in where the response surface has large derivatives
   - **Organ-target:** Sample near the iso-surface where D_arch is minimized for each organ
   - **Uncertainty-based:** Sample where the surrogate model has highest prediction variance
4. Combines into a single batch of ~200–400 additional runs

**HPC estimate:** 300 × 4 CPUs × ~4.5 h ≈ 5,400 CPU-hours (4% of monthly allocation).

**Total budget:** ~14,400 CPU-hours (10% of monthly allocation) — very feasible.

### Combined Analysis

After both stages, the full dataset (~700–900 runs) supports:
- High-fidelity surrogate models (Gaussian process or polynomial chaos)
- Global sensitivity analysis (Sobol, Morris)
- Organ-target optimization (which parameter combinations best match each organ?)
- Phase diagrams in reduced (2–3 factor) subspaces
