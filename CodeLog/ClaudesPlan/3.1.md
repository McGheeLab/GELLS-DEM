# GELS V3.1 — Well geometry, gravity, PMMA granules, contractile fibroblasts, cell division

Status: approved 2026-09-18; implementation in progress. Landed pieces are recorded in
`CodeLog/Updates/CHANGELOG.md` under `[V3.1]`.


## Context

The 21-condition 3D sweep (`results/sweep3d`: 2 mm cube, six rigid walls, 10 kPa hydrogel contact law,
constant-force cells, 72 h) showed far less compaction than the lab's experiment, and its 40 µm beds had
empty corners. The real system: **PMMA granules** (rigid, ρ ≈ 1180 kg/m³), collagen-coated and
**pre-seeded with human fibroblasts**, sedimented under **gravity** in a **well** (flat floor, cylindrical
wall, free top). Over 72 h the lab sees **void space between granules shrink and the bed pull away from an
inert boundary**; with a **functionalized boundary** there is no global compaction but a Cahn–Hilliard-like
coarsening in which the functional phase compacts and closes voids locally. Fibroblasts **divide**
(doubling ≈ 20–30 h). User decisions: simulate a **scaled 2 mm model well**; granules are pre-coated
(uniform t = 0 seeding, as now); **full scope** (Hill contractile bridge + division + geometry/gravity/PMMA
+ packing fix).

Root causes established by code reading and by re-measuring the sweep outputs:

1. **Packing artefact (problem 1).** The settle pulls every granule toward the box centre for the whole
   inflation — `attract = max(0.3*(1-t), 0.02)` at `gels/kernels/packing.py:346`, applied as a constant
   magnitude `attract·r_i` in `settle_sub_k` (:234-243); Python twin `gels/kernels/reference.py:641-650`
   (2D :419-427). Walls are a one-sided clamp (packing.py:267-277). For 20 µm-radius granules that is up to
   361 µm of inward drift: the 40 µm beds became a ball of radius ≈ 1400 µm in the 2000 µm cube — all eight
   200 µm corner cubes empty (0–1 granules vs 127 expected), interior 1.31× over-dense (φ_inner 0.725 > RCP),
   `post-relax hit 1000 steps, max_overlap=4.0 µm` in all seven 40 µm runs, `compaction_ratio` 1.13 at t = 0,
   and `phi_solid` 0.725 → 0.670 during the run: the bed relaxed *outward*, which `disp_func` (whole-domain
   average, `engine.py:2952-2963`) recorded as "compaction". RSA itself is uniform and complete.
2. **No compaction possible (problem 2).** (a) A closed six-wall box at fixed volume cannot compact globally
   at all. (b) The bridge is a constant-force actuator: `F = min(F_mc·maturity·alignment, F_max)`
   (reference.py:857-870, cells.py:583-592) with no dependence on gap, strain, velocity or load; the only
   thing that stops a cell is contact elasticity. (c) There is no gravity, no cylinder, no free surface
   anywhere (`boundary_mode` is only walls|periodic, `engine.py:94`). (d) PMMA stiffness cannot be used
   literally: explicit overdamped Euler with γ = drag_scale·r = 1 nN·h/µm and dt = 0.5 h is stable only for
   contact stiffness < 4 nN/µm, so non-penetration is really enforced by `_resolve_overlaps`
   (`gels/kernels/integrate.py`, `max_overlap_frac` 0.15) plus the `v_max` cap, not by Hertz forces.
   (e) Motor-clutch traction saturates monotonically in host E (`motor_clutch_force`, engine.py:1975-2015):
   46.6 nN at 10 kPa → 90.9 nN on 3 GPa with the template 100 nN stall — only 2×.
3. **No division (problem 3).** PROLIFERATING is a label; cell arrays are allocated once
   (`engine.py:463-487`), `gs.n_cells` is assigned once (:371); the count never changes.

**Physics bound to keep in view.** Rigid monodisperse spheres cannot pack past φ ≈ 0.64 (RCP); a sedimented
bed starts at 0.55–0.60. Densification alone can lower a bed by ≈ 7–15 %. Anything beyond that in the
experiment is envelope contraction (detachment from the wall, cluster coalescence) or cell/ECM volume — the
first two become representable only with a free top and a detachable side wall, which is what this plan adds.

## Part A — Well geometry, gravity, packing, PMMA (engine)

### A1. Free top and floor
- 3D: skip wall `w == 5` (lid) in `gather_3d` (`gels/kernels/contact3d.py:210`) and drop `(p.Lz, 2, -1)` from the
  walls list in `reference.compute_forces_3d` (:1824-1827). 2D: skip `w == 3` in `gather_2d`
  (`contact2d.py:226-234`) and the `p.Ly` entries at reference.py:1514-1519 / 1533-1538. Gravity acts along −y in 2D.
- `apply_position_bounds` as above; `n_top_clamped` reported in metrics/step 3 log (the neighbour grid
  `neighbors.py:184-207` and render grid `render.py:430-437` need positions inside the box).
- `generate_packing_2d_slice` slices at `bed_height_mean/2` instead of `Lz/2` when the top is free (engine.py:~2590).
- Template guidance: `Lz ≈ 1.4 × bed height` (headroom; taller wastes voxels).

### A2. Cylinder wall (3D only)
- Spheres: `rho = hypot(x-cx, y-cy)`, `pen = r_i + rho - R_cyl`, inward normal `(-ux, -uy, 0)`, JKR via
  `jkr_force_from_overlap(pen, r_i, wall_Estar[si], W_wall_i)`; clip plane `n = (ux, uy, 0)`, `d = R_cyl - rho`.
  Insert after the axis-wall loop in `gather_3d` and in reference.py:1818-1860; skip axes 0/1 when shape is cylinder.
  Under-estimate of the concave composite radius is 1–4 % (document).
- Superellipsoids: new `wall3d_plane_k(pos, a,b,c,n1,n2,quat,r_bound, plane_point, plane_normal)` in
  `gels/kernels/geometry3d.py` (next to `wall3d_k` :151, same 20×20 sampling) + reference twin
  `find_contact_wall_plane_3d` next to `find_contact_wall_3d` (engine.py:2510); the tangent plane at the granule's
  azimuth. Axis kernels untouched (legacy path identical).
- New kernel args: `gather_3d(..., shape_code, R_cyl, cx, cy, top_free, f_wall_unused?, ...)`,
  `gather_2d(..., top_free, ...)`; drivers `compute_forces_3d`/`compute_forces` pass `boundary_geometry(p)`.
- RSA (`engine.py:2277-2295`): reject `(cx-cxc)²+(cy-cyc)² > (R_cyl - r_bound - gap)²`. The shared `RSAChecker`
  keeps compiled and reference RSA bit-identical.
- `species_counts` (engine.py:1347), `generate_packing_3d` (:2208, :2356) and metrics `V_domain` use `domain_volume(p)`.
- Amount spec: `granules.bed_height_um` (0 = off) with `granules.bed_solid_fraction` (assumed φ_bed 0.60 for the

### A4. Packing consolidation (the fix for problem 1)
- `settle_sub_k` (packing.py:194-278) gains `g_mag, gx, gy, gz, gw, shape_code, R_cyl, cx, cy, mobile` and returns
  `(max_ov, max_disp)`. Body force `g_mag·gw[i]·(gx,gy,gz)` is added at the same position as the legacy attract
  term (so the reference's vectorised `np.add.at` order matches); radial clamp for the cylinder; fixed granules
  (`mobile[i] == False`) are not moved. Legacy call (`g_mag = 0`, `shape_code = 0`, all mobile) executes exactly
  today's statements. Reference twins `_settle_packing_3d` (reference.py:580-805) / `_settle_packing_2d`
  (:370-577): `mode = consolidation_mode(p)`; `centre` keeps the legacy attract; `gravity` applies `fz -= g·gw`
  (fy in 2D); `none` applies neither.
- Vertical placement for `gravity`: `V_solid = phi_solid_target·domain_volume(p)`,
  `H_bed = V_solid/(bed_solid_fraction·A_base)`, `H_place = min(Lz, max(H_bed, 4·r_max))`,
  `alpha = clip((phi_safe/(V_solid/(A_base·H_place)))^(1/dim), 0.3, 1)`; sample `z ∈ [rb+gap, H_place-rb-gap]`.
  Placing the deflated granules inside the final bed height lets inflation do the compaction and leaves gravity to
  reseat rattlers and flatten the surface.
- Schedule (settle units; `gw_i = (r_target_i/mean r)³`, `n_layers = H_place/(2·mean r)`):
  inflation `g_hi = 0.35·k_rep·mean_r^1.5/n_layers` (bottom-layer equilibrium overlap ≈ 0.5·mean_r, early exit
  disabled); post-relax `g_k = max(g_hi·0.7^(k//20), g_lo)`, `g_lo = 0.25·k_rep·(0.05·mean_r)^1.5/n_layers`, cap
  2000 steps, stop when `max_overlap < tol` and `g_k == g_lo` and `max_disp < 0.02·mean_r`. Tune against the
  acceptance test (φ_bed 0.55–0.64, overlaps < tol, ≤ 1 % floating).
- Step 2 report (`pipeline/step2_pack.py:127-168`): bed height mean/p95 vs estimate, `phi_bed`, `n_floating`,
  `wall_contact_fraction`, corner occupancy for boxes; `save_params_metadata` gains `boundary` and `bed_height_um`.
- Bug fix rationale goes in the CHANGELOG with the measured numbers from Context §1.

### A5. PMMA material
- `build_pair_tables(..., E_cap_kPa=0.0)` (`gels/materials.py:174-213`): `E_eff = min(E, E_cap)` for
  `pair_Estar`/`wall_Estar` only; `gs.E_gran` keeps the true modulus (motor-clutch, spreading factor).
- Coulomb friction in `pair_pass_3d` (contact3d.py:118), `pair_pass_2d` (contact2d.py:121), reference.py:1441/:1758:
  `F_t_cap = tau_0·A·1e-3 + mu·max(F_n, 0)`; `F_fric = F_t_cap·tanh(vt/v_ref)`. Additive: for rigid granules
  τ₀·A ≈ 1e-3 nN so a `min` would switch friction off; `mu = 0` skips the branch (bit-identical).
- Recommended preset values (calibrate; see D): cap 100 kPa (Hertz force at the 0.6 µm tolerance ≈ 100 nN, the
  force scale the engine is calibrated at), `max_overlap_frac 0.03`, `mc_dem.enabled false`, `friction_mu 0.3`,
  `adhesion_energy {cc 5e-4, cb 2e-4, bb 1e-4}` (template values give a granule–wall JKR pull-off of cell-force
  scale, which would pin granules to an inert wall), gravity on, consolidation auto, cylinder + free top.
- Wall Coulomb friction: **deferred** — the `+0.5 µm` clip inset means the wall's repulsive branch never fires, so
  there is no normal force to multiply; revisit with a floor-reaction model.

### A6. Boundary functionalization
- `boundary.functionalization` (f_wall, default 0): `wall_W[a] = mix_pair(f[a], f_wall, W_cc, W_cb, W_bb)` in
  `build_pair_tables` (f_wall = 0 reduces to `mix_wall` exactly).
- `boundary.layer: {enabled false, radius_um null (→ smallest mobile species mean), embed_frac 0.5, seed_cells true}`:
  the packer appends a synthetic species `boundary` (f = f_wall, colour grey, `fixed: true`) and places a hexagonal
  lattice on the floor (centres at `z = r_w·(1-2·embed_frac)`) and on the side wall (cylinder ring lattice / the two
  2D side walls) BEFORE RSA, adding them to the `RSAChecker`. `gs.fixed` (N,) bool: exempt from integration
  (`vel[fixed] = 0` in `step()`), from `apply_position_bounds`, from gravity, from inflation (keep target radii), and
  the mobile partner takes the full correction in `resolve_overlaps_k` / `_resolve_overlaps` (new `mobile` arg);
  excluded from counts, `phi_*_true`, displacement metrics and `n_granules` (reported separately as `n_boundary`).
  Cells seeded on layer granules via the existing seeding law; bridging to/from them needs no new code. Saved in
  snapshots as `fixed`; viz colours from the species table.

## Part B — Cell model (engine)

### B1. Contractile (Hill) bridge — `bridge_force_model: constant | hill`
Per candidate pair (i, j) with committed bridges b (both directions), computed in `bridge_pairs_k`
(`gels/kernels/cells.py:457-521`, which already loops both sides' cells) and in a prelude of
`_service_committed_bridges` (reference.py:833-895):
```
F_s,b  = min(F_c,side·maturity_b·alignment_b, F_max)          # today's per-cell force
F_iso  = Σ F_s,b ; F_prev = Σ cell_bridge_force[b]
c_pair = 1/(drag_scale·r_i) + 1/(drag_scale·r_j)
v_rel  = (gap_prev − gap)/dt   (0 if gap_prev is NaN)          # closing speed
v_env  = v_rel − c_pair·F_prev                                 # closing imposed by everything else
fac    = clip((1 − v_env/v_0)/(1 + c_pair·F_iso/v_0), 0, 1 + f_ecc);  fac = 1 if gap <= bridge_min_gap
F_b    = fac·F_s,b   along ±n as today
```
Limits (unit-tested): rigid environment → fac = 1 (isometric); free pair → closes at `v_0·x/(1+x)` with no
oscillation (a naive explicit `1 − v/v_0` limit-cycles with loop gain ≈ 15 at template numbers); stretched →
up to `1+f_ecc`; `v_0 → ∞` → fac = 1.0 exactly (= `constant`).
- New per-cell arrays: `cell_bridge_gap_prev` (float64, NaN) and `cell_bridge_force` (float64, 0), written at
  every service/commit for both models, reset on rupture; saved/restored with defaults.
- Six application sites: reference.py:857-870, :880-893, :1108-1122, :1202-1215; cells.py:583-592, :694-704.
  `_service_committed_bridges` returns `(n_ci, n_cj, fac)`; callers reference.py:1352/1486/1691/1804 and
  `gels/kernels/bridging.py:171` pass `pair_fac` into `_attempt_new_bridges`. Kernel: `out_fac (Mc,)` from
  `bridge_pairs_k`, consumed by `bridge_cells_k`; `out_fac = 1.0` when model = 0 (bit-identical).
- Params: `bridge_force_model 'constant'`, `cell_contraction_speed 12.0 µm/h` (range 6–30; 0.1–0.5 µm/min
  stress-fibre shortening `[verify]`), `bridge_eccentric_gain 0.5`, `bridge_min_gap 0.0 µm`.
  YAML `cells.bridging.{force_model, contraction_speed_um_per_h, eccentric_gain, min_gap_um}`.
- With `hill`: preset `v_max 30 µm/h` (≥ 2·v_0) and `dt 0.25 h`. Lock-in occurs once load-bearing at contact.

## Risks and expectations
- Rigidity is geometric (projection + v_max): forces on loaded contacts are overshoots, not equilibrium forces;
  per-step jitter up to the overlap tolerance. Documented; `drag_scale` 1–2.5 is an optional experiment.
- Empty headspace lowers voxels per granule (render grid spans Lz) — keep Lz ≈ 1.4·H_bed.
- Kernel signature changes recompile once (`ensure_cache_fresh`); bit-identity of the new packer mode requires the
  body-force term at the legacy accumulation position and the ckdtree backend in tests.
- The fixed boundary layer adds ~12 % granules at 40 µm and makes the wall rough; compare f_wall = 0 with and
  without the layer to separate roughness from cell-mediated adhesion.
- Expected physics: with a free top the bed height can fall by ≈ 7–15 % from densification and the bed can detach
  from an inert wall; a functionalized wall pins it and the functional phase coarsens locally. Experimental
  compaction well beyond ≈ 15 % of rigid-sphere rearrangement cannot be reproduced without granule loss, a much
  looser initial bed (test by lowering `bed_solid_fraction`), or cell/ECM volume, which the model does not represent.
