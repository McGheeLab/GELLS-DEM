# V2.3: Bridge Exclusion Zones + Post-Bridge Alignment

## Context

Two issues with bridge formation behavior:
1. Cells migrated quickly across a granule to form bridges right next to existing bridges,
   rather than spreading out. Real fibroblasts would avoid bridging near existing bridges
   and instead crawl around the granule to find unoccupied regions.
2. After forming bridges, fibroblasts should continue to align stress fibers and elongate
   along the bridge axis to maximize force output. The old model had only a maturity ramp
   (adhesion formation, 0-2h) but no continued force amplification from alignment.

## Feature 1: Bridge Exclusion Zone

**Problem**: Multiple cells pile up at the same contact point, forming redundant bridges.
**Solution**: Angular exclusion check — cells skip bridging if existing BRIDGING/MIGRATING
cells on the same granule→target pair are within `bridge_exclusion_angle` (0.8 rad ~ 46 deg).

### Implementation:
- Before per-cell loop in `_attempt_new_bridges`, collect angular positions of all
  BRIDGING/MIGRATING cells on gi targeting gj (and vice versa for gj→gi).
- For each candidate cell, compute angular separation from all existing bridges:
  - 2D: `min(|theta_c - theta_k|, 2pi - |theta_c - theta_k|)`
  - 3D: `arccos(dot(p_c, p_k))` on parametric unit sphere
- If any separation < `bridge_exclusion_angle`, skip: cell prefers to crawl around.

### New Params:
- `bridge_exclusion_angle: float = 0.8` -- rad (~46 deg), min angular separation

## Feature 2: Post-Bridge Stress Fiber Alignment

**Problem**: Bridge force instantly reaches F_mc after maturity ramp. Real fibroblasts
continue to remodel and align for hours after initial bridge formation.
**Solution**: Per-cell `cell_alignment` field modulates bridge force output.

### Model:
```
alignment(t) = 1 - (1 - alignment_min) * exp(-alignment_rate * t)
F_bridge = F_mc * maturity * alignment
```

- Initialized at `bridge_alignment_min` (0.3) when entering BRIDGING
- Grows toward 1.0 at rate `bridge_alignment_rate` (0.3 /h)
- Two-stage force profile:
  - Phase 1 (0-2h): maturity ramp 0→1 (adhesion formation)
  - Phase 2 (2-10h): alignment 0.3→1.0 (stress fiber reorientation)
  - Total time to full force: ~8-10h (matches real fibroblast remodeling)
- Resets to 0 on bridge rupture or senescence

### Implementation:
- New per-cell array: `cell_alignment` (float64, 0-1)
- `_update_individual_cells` BRIDGING handler: updates alignment each dt
- `_service_committed_bridges`: `F = F_mc * maturity * alignment`
- BRIDGING entry points (3 locations): initialize to `bridge_alignment_min`
- MIGRATING entry: initialize to 0
- Bridge rupture/senescence (5 locations): reset to 0
- Added to snapshot serialization

### New Params:
- `bridge_alignment_rate: float = 0.3` -- 1/h
- `bridge_alignment_min: float = 0.3` -- initial factor

## Verification (completed 2026-03-17)

Ran 12h 2D deformable trial (`results/test_exclusion_alignment/`):
- Bridges spread spatially around granules (no stacking at same contact)
- Bridge count builds gradually: 0→23 over 12h
- Alignment at t=12h: mean=0.871, min=0.300 (new bridges), max=0.977 (fully aligned)
- Average bridge force ramps: 0→0.7→2.1→4.6→5.8→7.8→9.9 nN (alignment effect visible)
- Force profile matches expected two-stage buildup (maturity + alignment)
